FAQ

Why is the result's text not selectable?

To shrink already-compressed files, pages are rebuilt as optimized images. That is what makes big reductions possible.

What if it can't reach my target?

You'll get the smallest version possible, clearly marked, so you can choose a slightly larger target.

Is there a file size limit?

Files up to 100 MB. Very long documents take longer because every page is processed.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, but large PDFs compress faster on a laptop.
